# Privacy Policy – BibleWish

Effective Date: January 2026 (updated November 2026, for optional sign-in)

BibleWish respects your privacy. You can use the whole app without an account, and then it does
not collect, store, track, or share any personal data: it all stays on your device.

## Without an account

All reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading plans, prayers, achievements, and
preferences are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## If you choose to sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets your notes, favorite verses, highlights, reading progress, reading
plans, and private prayer list follow you between this app and biblewish.com. If you sign in, we
store on our server:

- your email address (to send you a sign-in code and to identify your account), and an account ID
- the notes, favorites, highlights, reading progress, plans, and private prayers you save

This is used only to run those features. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, used for
tracking, or shown to anyone else. Your prayer list and notes are private. You can export
everything we keep, or delete your account and all of it, at any time from Settings → Account.
Signing out leaves what is already on your device.

## Notifications and Widget

If you turn on the daily verse reminder, BibleWish schedules notifications on your device. They are
created and delivered locally; no notification data is sent anywhere. The Verse of the Day widget
reads the day's verse from files bundled with the app, and shares only your chosen Bible
translation between the app and the widget on your device.

## Third-Party Services

BibleWish does not use third-party services, analytics, advertising networks,
or tracking technologies.

## Children’s Privacy

BibleWish does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
The app is safe to use for all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of BibleWish ever change, this Privacy Policy will be
updated accordingly.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on
the project’s GitHub repository.
