# BibleWish App Redesign — Plan

Status: **Phases 0–3, polish, Phase 5 and Phase 4 (tier B) built (2026-11-22); on-device testing and website deployment pending** · Created 2026-11-22

Goal: bring the iOS app in line with the identity and features of biblewish.com (source: `../BibleWish_Website`, see its `docs/PORTAL_PLAN.md` and `CLAUDE.md`).

## Decisions (2026-11-22)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Scope | Phases 0–3: look + Home + Plans + Study, all on-device |
| Accounts / sync | Not now. Revisit in Phase 4. Privacy stays "no data leaves the device" |
| Website data | Bundled copies of the website's JSON (offline, simple) |
| Minimum iOS | 17.5 (already the project target), so `@Observable` is allowed |

## Design language (from the website)

- Dark by default: page `#0b0a09` with a warm radial gradient (`#2a1f1a` → `#0b0a09`); panels `#17120e`.
- Gold accent `#c7a96b` (bright `#e0ba78`), gold hairlines at ~13% opacity.
- Scripture in a serif (Iowan Old Style / Palatino → SwiftUI `.serif` design); UI in the system sans.
- Themes: Dark / Sepia / Light / Auto. Paper themes darken the accents.
- Reader extras: "book page" frame (double gold border, ornaments), illuminated drop cap on verse 1, focus mode, reading time, red letters.
- Every color comes from a token; no hard-coded colors in views.

## Working notes

## Phase 4 — accounts and sync (tier B, built 2026-11-22)

Optional sign-in to biblewish.com (email code), then favorites, notes, highlights, reading progress, reading plans and a new private prayer list stay in step with the website. Decisions: tier B; highlight colors stored on the server. Details: `PHASE4_PLAN.md`, `PHASE4_SERVER.md`.

- **Offline first:** the device is the source of truth; every change is queued (`SyncOp`) and sent first, and only then is the server's state pulled. Signed out, the app behaves exactly as before.
- **Safe by construction:** a plan or the highlights are overwritten or removed by the server only after it has acknowledged them; a different account starts clean; a sync request during a pass waits and goes round again.
- **Website changes** (tested locally, **not committed or deployed**): highlight colors (migration 017), a reading import and a plans import that keep real dates and make no read events today.
- **Account screen:** export my data, sign out, delete account (typed confirmation), sync status and Sync now.
- **Privacy:** manifest, `PRIVACY.md` and the App Store label draft now describe optional sign-in (email, user id, user content, reading progress).
- **Tests:** a Debug-only harness (`BibleWish/Sync/SelfTest.swift`) signs in through the local server and runs 117 checks, in the shipping Release build it isn't present.
- **Found and fixed on the way:** progress counting ignored real chapter keys (`kjv|John-3`), so Home, Bible progress and the reading badges had counted nothing for real reading history.

Not checked: running against production (the website changes aren't deployed), the real email delivery, and every flow on a physical device.

## Note formatting (2026-11-22)

Notes use the website's syntax: `# headings`, `**bold**`, `*italic*` / `_italic_`, bullets (`-`, `*`, `•`), numbers (`1.` or `1)`), `> quotes`, `[links](https://…)`, `{red|colored words}`, `{=gold|highlighted words}`, boxes (`::: gold Title` … `:::`) and verse cards (`::: verse John 3:16 · KJV` … `:::`). The inline part is a port of the website's own pattern (`NoteInline`), and Debug builds check it against the website's real regex on 54 tricky lines (`node tools/gen_note_fixtures.mjs` regenerates the cases; run it when the website's syntax changes). Where the website is quirky the app matches it on purpose, for example `[**bold**](url)` shows the asterisks inside a link, and `***text***` reads as bold `*text` followed by `*`. The Study list and the title of an untitled note show plain words, not markup.



## Note editor toolbar (2026-11-22)

The editor has the website's formatting actions: bold, italic, heading, bullets, numbers, quote, link, text color, highlighter, colored box, Insert a verse and a preview. The actions are pure functions (`NoteEditing`, a port of the website's `attachNoteTools`) tested without any screen; the text box is a `UITextView` (SwiftUI's `TextEditor` can't expose the selection on iOS 17) so the toolbar can act on the selection and keep undo. Return in a list starts the next item. Insert a verse works offline in any translation (the verse text comes from the app's own files, not `api/passage.php`) and writes the same `::: verse John 3:16 · KJV` card the website does.

Not checked: typing and tapping the toolbar on a device (the logic and the text-view integration are covered by the Debug self-test, 176 checks).

## Book selection like the website (2026-11-22)

The Bible tab is the website's navigator (read.html: BOOK_GROUPS, the navigator sheet): a search field that also takes a reference ("John 3:16" opens it, a book name filters, no match offers "Search the Bible for…"), recent chapters, Old / New Testament tabs, colored section chips that jump to a section, and the ten sections (Law, History, Poetry & Wisdom, Major and Minor Prophets, Gospels, Acts, Letters of Paul, General Letters, Prophecy) each with its own color. Books are tinted tiles with the section color along the left edge; the book being read glows; single-chapter books open the reader directly. Chapters are a grid of square tiles in the book's color with a check on read chapters. Tapping the reader's title opens the same navigator as a sheet and switches the chapter in place. On the paper themes the section colors are darkened for text (the site's `--group-darken`: 58% Sepia, 55% Light).

Kept from the old screen: a hairline on each book tile shows how much of it you have read (the website puts progress on the Plans page; the app keeps it on the tile as well as in Plans). Dropped: the logo and the OT/NT headings with emoji.

Not checked: tapping through the navigator on a device (the layout was checked in Dark and Sepia on iPhone and in Dark on iPad; the section, search and recents logic is covered by the Debug self-test).


## Selecting verses in the reader (2026-11-22)

Redone after the website's reader (read.html: the selection bar). Before: a tap toggled a favorite with no sign of it, and a long press opened a plain dialog. Now a tap selects a verse (a highlight in the pen color, with a colored edge) and tapping again deselects; a long press selects every verse from the nearest selected one to this one (a range). While anything is selected a bar rises from the bottom with the reference written the website's way (`John 3:16-18,21`), the four pen colors and these actions: Save (a favorite per run of verses; the heart fills when they are all saved and tapping again removes them), Note (opens the note editor on the passage), Image (Sunrise / Field / Sunset / Night / Gold, Square or Story, drawn on the device and shared), Study (Related verses, Places in this chapter), Copy, Share (reference, translation, numbered verses and a link, as on the website) and Clear (with Undo). Favorited verses carry a small heart. In focus mode the first tap still brings the verse numbers back.

Send and Original words follow below. Not checked: tapping through the bar and the share sheet on a device (layout checked in Dark and Sepia; the logic, the reference wording against the website's own function on 14 selections, favorites per run and Clear/Undo are covered by the Debug self-test, 209 checks).

### Send to someone and Original words (2026-11-22)

**Original words** (Study menu in the bar): each KJV word of up to five selected verses with the Hebrew or Greek behind it (lemma, transliteration, a short meaning); tap a word for the full entry (Strong's number, pronunciation, meaning, KJV renderings, origin, times used). The data is the website's own (`data/strongs`, 9.4 MB, bundled so it works offline; `tools/build_strongs.py` copies it and checks every number has an entry). The words are the KJV's, whatever translation is open, as on the website.

**Send** (bar): a name, a message, a background, an optional email and your name; the server (`api/verses.php`, deployed) makes a private link `biblewish.com/v/<token>` that you send by WhatsApp, Messages or anything the share sheet offers, or copy. A member at the email address also gets it in their notifications on the website. It needs an account (the sheet says so and offers Sign in); a message covers the first to the last selected verse, up to 30. The sender's notifications (opened, answered) appear on the website's bell; the app has no notification list yet. Both are hidden when the book can't be placed in the 66-book order (some Hebrew-order books).

Debug self-test: 230 checks, including a live round trip (the server makes a link and reads it back; refuses a bad email). Not checked: tapping through the sheets and the share sheet on a device; opening a sent link in a browser.
