# BibleWish 

<img width="128" height="128" alt="logo_128" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/21784f4e-d0df-4ba4-9230-77000f54b041" />


BibleWish was created with a simple and uncompromising purpose:

To make the Word of God freely accessible, without distraction, monetization,
or commercial interest.

This project is not a product, a startup, or a platform for profit.
It is a service — offered freely, as a gift — in obedience to the principle:

> “Freely you have received; freely give.” (Matthew 10:8)


### ✝️ Vision

BibleWish is designed to help people read, track, and engage with the Bible in
a clean, focused, and respectful way. There are no ads, no paid features, no
hidden monetization, and no intention to commercialize Scripture.

The source code is open for learning, study, and contribution so that others
may grow — technically and spiritually — but it may not be used for commercial
purposes.

If BibleWish helps even one person read the Bible more faithfully, more
consistently, or with greater understanding, then its purpose is fulfilled.

### What’s inside

- **Reading:** a book-style reader with a serif page, an illuminated first letter,
  focus mode, reading time, adjustable text, and the words of Jesus in red.
- **Home:** a verse for every day, today’s reading from your plans, and where you
  left off.
- **Plans:** reading plans (the Bible in a year, the New Testament, the Gospels,
  Psalms, Proverbs) with progress, streaks, and progress through every book.
- **Study:** favorite verses, notes with colored tags and light formatting (verse
  references open the passage), and a private prayer list; cross-references;
  places and journeys on a map.
- **Achievements:** badges that celebrate faithfulness. Nothing is ranked.
- **Daily verse:** an optional reminder and a Home Screen / Lock Screen widget.
- **Themes:** Dark, Sepia, Light, or follow the device.

The design, data and plans come from [biblewish.com](https://biblewish.com).
Everything works on your device. Signing in is optional and keeps your notes,
favorites, highlights, progress, plans and prayers in step with the website. See [`docs/REDESIGN_PLAN.md`](docs/REDESIGN_PLAN.md)
for how the app was rebuilt around the website, and [`docs/WIDGET.md`](docs/WIDGET.md)
for the widget.

### Bible Translation Support

BibleWish includes the **King James Version (KJV)** and **World English Bible
(WEBU)** in English, **Biblia Livre (BLIV)** in Portuguese, and a **Hebrew Bible**.

The KJV text is in the public domain, which allows it to be freely used, shared,
and distributed without licensing restrictions. This aligns with the core mission
of BibleWish: providing free and unrestricted access to God’s Word.

Any new translations added will be limited to versions that are legally open and
free to distribute, respecting both copyright law and the integrity of Scripture.

Our goal is to expand translation support responsibly, while preserving the
principle of free access for everyone.
